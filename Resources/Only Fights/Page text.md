Overview:
Only Fights is a 1-2 player co-op beat 'em up where you play as starting out social media influencer. The only way to grow your following however, is to beat up all the other social media influencer and broadcast it live to your audience. As you navigate this insane world, you are met with comedic enemies, hidden background gags, and a compelling story about social media and the choices influencers must make.

My Role

As Creative Director, I am responsible for not only the direction of the game's content, but also act as the leader for a team of 20+ individuals, all working on a volunteer basis. Leading meetings, delegating tasks, and keeping my team motivated has been a truly rewarding experience. 

I have been fortunate enough to work with some truly talented and remarkable people during my time as Creative Director of Only Fights and I am proud of every team member on the project!

Responsibilities

Creative Vision — defined and protected the project's core vision from concept through release, keeping every feature, art direction, and tone decision aligned with it

Team Leadership — led a multidisciplinary team of designers, artists, and developers, setting the creative direction and culture for the project

Game Design — shaped the core combat mechanics and player-facing systems that bring the vision to life, iterating on them through playtesting

Narrative Lead — authored the story, settings, and comic scripts that give the project its social media and comedic voice

Challenges

Volunteer Student Team
As all members of the team are volunteers and not being financially compensated for their role on the team, this can lead to production issues when members must step away for periods of time due to class, work, or other outside commitment.
Solutions: 
1. Know what is needed and one can be cut from the project.
2. Create a culture of communication. Team members always feel that it is safe to communicate other priorities.
3. If something is needed, know how and where to distribute the work

Limited Time and Scope
The project's development cycle has a hard deadline. We are given 2 semesters to have a complete experience, which includes creating, testing, and polishing the game experience.
Solutions:
1. Have hard deadlines for features. If the feature can't be made by the deadline, it must be cut.
2. Be able to either reuse assets whenever possible and make limitations your strength
3. Never wait for testing a feature. Get it in front of people to identify possible issues early.

Mechanic and Theme Alignment
The central mechanic of Only Fights is a chat based health system. The more that chat loves you, the more health you have. Because this is a unique system, player comprehension is always at the forefront of our minds.
Solutions:
1. Test constantly. We are testing once a week and working out how we can change our mechanic for the better
2. Actively listen to playtesters and ask questions based on their experiences.
3. Never force a mechanic that is not working. we want to adapt our mechanic around our theme, not the other way around.
