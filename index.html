Who I Am

About Me

I am a project-oriented game designer who thrives at the intersection of design, storytelling, and production. With experience spanning game design, game writing, and production, I bring a fresh perspective to every project I interact with.

I am currently a 3rd year graduate student at the University of Southern California, pursuing a Master of Fine Arts in Games and Interactive Media.

Outside of Work

In addition to making games, I am also a personal fan of many game genres and styles. A few of my favorites are TMNT: Shredder's Revenge, Warhammer 40k: Space Marines 2, and Helldivers 2. I am also an avid player of tabletop and card games! I love playing Magic: The Gathering with my friends on the weekends or Dungeons and Dragons when our schedules can line up for it. If not, I am always down to play something else like Settlers of Catan, Betrayal at the House on the Hill, or any other game someone brings over.

There is not a game I won't try or an experience I will turn down!

In addition to games, I love to go to music events and festivals, spend time with my girlfriend, and attend sports games. I grew up in San Jose, California, so I am a lifelong fan of the San Jose Sharks and Las Vegas Raiders. When not at games, I love exploring new music and venues around Los Angeles with friends.
